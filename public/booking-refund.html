Booking, cancellation & refund policy

Effective 16 August 2026 · Jamin Properties I PVT LTD

This policy explains how a plot booking works on Jamin Bazaar, how you pay, and what happens if you cancel. It forms part of the Terms of Use.

How a booking works

  1. Reserve. You select a plot in the app and place a booking request. The plot is held provisionally; it is not yet yours.
  2. Complete KYC. Upload your identity and address proof. A booking cannot be confirmed without it.
  3. Pay outside the app. The booking screen shows the booking amount and the bank account of Jamin Properties I PVT LTD. Pay by bank transfer, NEFT/RTGS/IMPS or UPI. No payment is taken inside the app.
  4. Upload proof. Enter the transaction reference or UTR and upload the receipt.
  5. Verification. We match the payment against your booking, normally within 2 working days, and issue a written confirmation. The booking status in the app updates accordingly.
  6. Agreement and registration. The sale agreement, payment schedule and registration follow, as set out in your written booking confirmation.

Payment safety. The account name must read Jamin Properties I PVT LTD. We never ask you to pay into an individual's personal account, and we never ask for your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, net-banking password or OTP. If anyone claiming to represent Jamin Bazaar asks for these, stop and contact us.

What the booking amount is

The booking amount is an advance towards the total consideration for the plot. It is stated in rupees on the booking screen before you pay and is repeated in your booking confirmation. Stamp duty, registration charges, documentation charges, GST where applicable, and any development or maintenance charges are separate and are set out in the sale agreement.

Cancellation by you

When you cancelWhat happens
Before your payment is verified The reservation lapses. If a payment was made and has not yet been applied, it is returned in full.
Within 7 days of booking confirmation, before the sale agreement is signed The booking amount is refunded after deduction of documented administrative costs actually incurred, as itemised to you in writing.
After 7 days but before the sale agreement is signed Refund is subject to the deduction stated in your booking confirmation, which is disclosed before you pay.
After the sale agreement is signed Governed by the cancellation and forfeiture clauses of that agreement, not by this policy.

To cancel, email info@jaminbazaar.in from your registered email with your booking reference, or use My Bookings → Cancel booking in the app.

Cancellation by us

We may cancel a booking if KYC is not completed within the stated period, if payment is not received or cannot be verified, if the documents submitted are found to be false, or if the parcel becomes unavailable for reasons outside our control such as a title defect or a regulatory restriction. Where we cancel for a reason that is not your fault, the full amount you paid is refunded without deduction.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are made only to the bank account from which the payment originated, in the name of the person who booked.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 10 working days of approval. Credit to your account depends on your bank.
  • Refunds are made in Indian rupees. Bank charges levied by your own bank are not reimbursed.
  • No interest is payable on refunded amounts unless required by law.

Disputes

Raise any dispute with our Grievance Officer at info@jaminbazaar.in. We acknowledge within 24 hours and aim to resolve within 15 days. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 or the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
