Privacy Policy

Applies to the Jamin Bazaar mobile app (app.jamindar.mobile) and this website. Last updated 5 August 2026.

Jamin Bazaar is operated by Jamin Property Developers. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, who it reaches and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. Who we are

Jamin Property Developers (“Jamin”, “we”, “us”) publishes verified plotted developments and operates the Jamin Bazaar app for buyers and promoters. We are the data controller for the information described below.

2. What we collect

We collect only what a feature actually needs. Nothing below is collected in the background for its own sake.

Account and profile

  • Mobile number — this is your identity on Jamin Bazaar. Sign-in is by one-time password; we never ask you for a password.
  • Name, email, city, district, state and preferred language — so the app and our team can address you properly and show relevant projects.
  • Gender and date of birth — optional, and only if you enter them.
  • Profile photograph — optional, and removable at any time from your profile or digital card.

Identity and payout details (KYC) — only if you choose to verify

A buyer can use the whole app without ever submitting KYC. A promoter needs it to become a Verified Jamin Partner and to be paid commission. If and only if you submit it, we collect:

  • PAN number and a photograph of the PAN card
  • Aadhaar number and photographs of the front and back
  • Courier address
  • Bank account name, number, IFSC, bank and branch, a bank proof image, and UPI ID — used solely to pay commission owed to you
  • Nominee details, if you provide them

These documents are stored in a private, non-public bucket. They are never served on a public link. They are readable only by you and by an authorised Jamin administrator reviewing your verification, and each view is opened through a short-lived signed link rather than a permanent address.

Content you create

  • Community posts, comments and any photos, videos, documents or voice notes you attach
  • Properties you submit for review as a promoter, including photos, videos and documents
  • Messages you send through the in-app inbox, and their attachments
  • Enquiries, callback requests, site-visit bookings and saved projects

Device permissions

Each of these is requested at the moment you use the feature, never on launch, and the app works without granting them:

PermissionUsed for
CameraTaking a photo of a KYC document, a property, or a community post.
Photos & mediaChoosing an existing photo or video to upload.
MicrophoneSpeaking to the Jamindar assistant, and recording voice notes.
Location (approximate or precise)Showing projects near you, and tagging the location of a property a promoter submits. We do not track your location in the background.

Voice you send to the assistant

Jamindar is a voice and text property advisor. When you speak to it, the recording is sent to our speech provider to be transcribed, and the transcript, its English translation and the assistant's reply are stored so the conversation can continue and be reviewed for quality. If you would rather not have this stored, use the assistant by typing instead.

Usage and attribution

  • Which projects you open, save, enquire about or book a visit for
  • The referral or invite code that brought you to Jamin Bazaar, so the promoter who introduced you is credited
  • An activity log of significant actions, used for support and for detecting abuse

We do not run advertising, we do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Why we collect it

  • To run your account — signing you in, showing your saved projects, visits and messages.
  • To answer you — connecting an enquiry to a Jamin partner who can call you back.
  • To verify partners and pay them — the KYC and bank details above.
  • To keep the community civil — contact details posted in the community are automatically masked, and reports are reviewed.
  • To improve the product — in aggregate, not by reading your private messages.

A Jamin promoter you enquire with will see your name and the contact details you chose to share with them, so they can respond. We will also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile — until you ask us to delete the account.
  • KYC documents — for as long as you are a verified partner, and afterwards only as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • One-time passwords — minutes; they expire and are discarded.
  • Community posts — until you delete them or the account.

6. Your choices

  • See and correct your details in the app under Account → Edit profile.
  • Remove your profile photo at any time — the stored file is deleted, not just hidden.
  • Delete your account and data — see Delete your account.
  • Decline any permission — the app keeps working; only that feature stops.
  • Ask us anything about your data at info@jaminproperties.com.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to records is enforced at the database itself, row by row, so one member's account cannot read another's. KYC documents sit in a private bucket reachable only through short-lived signed links. No system is perfect, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects you.

8. Children

Jamin Bazaar is intended for adults buying, selling or promoting property. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects you, tell you in the app.
