Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026 · Applies to the Jamin Bazaar mobile application (app.jamindar.mobile) and jaminbazaar.in

This policy explains what personal information Jamin Properties I PVT LTD (“we”, “us”) collects through the Jamin Bazaar app and website, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and Google Play's User Data policy.

We collect only what is needed to show you land parcels, arrange site visits, record a booking, meet identity-verification obligations and complete a property transaction.

We do not collect payments inside the app. Booking amounts are paid directly to Jamin Properties I PVT LTD by bank transfer or UPI using the details shown in your booking. We never ask for and never store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, net-banking password or OTP. Anyone requesting these in our name is not acting for us.

What we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. We do not collect biometric data, health data, caste, religion, or political affiliation. We do not store full Aadhaar numbers or unmasked Aadhaar images; where Aadhaar is used for verification, only the last four digits are retained.

3. How we use your information

  • To register you and keep your account secure, including one-time passwords sent to your mobile or email.
  • To show plot listings, prices, approvals and availability.
  • To schedule and confirm site visits.
  • To record a booking, verify your identity, and confirm your payment against it.
  • To send transactional messages about your visits, bookings and payment status.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to investigate suspected fraud.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations relating to real-estate transactions.
  • To fix defects and improve the app using aggregated, non-identifying usage data.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making that produces legal effects, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary, and only with:

  • Service providers who host and run the platform on our instructions — cloud hosting and database, SMS and email delivery, crash reporting. They may not use your data for their own purposes.
  • The land owner or promoter of the parcel you book, and their legal representatives, where this is necessary to execute the sale agreement or registration.
  • Professional advisers — advocates, chartered accountants, document writers — engaged for your transaction.
  • Government authorities, including the Registration Department, RERA and tax authorities, where required by law or by the transaction itself.
  • Law enforcement or courts, where we are legally compelled.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Where your data is stored

Your information is stored on managed cloud infrastructure. Identity documents and payment proofs are held in private storage that is not publicly reachable and is accessible only through short-lived authenticated links to our authorised staff. Where any processing takes place outside India, it is subject to contractual safeguards and to the restrictions in force under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

When a retention period ends, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

7. Your rights

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
  • Erase your data and delete your account — see Delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for anything we do on the basis of consent. Withdrawing consent for KYC means we cannot process a booking.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer, and to the Data Protection Board of India.

Write to info@jaminbazaar.in to exercise any of these. We respond within 30 days.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app — open the Account tab and tap Delete account — or from this website without installing the app. That page sets out exactly what is deleted, what is retained, and for how long.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses TLS. Sign-in is by one-time passwords that expire quickly and are rate-limited; we never ask you for a password. Access to identity documents is restricted to authorised staff and is logged. Uploaded files are validated by type and size. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as required.

10. Children

Jamin Bazaar is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, write to us and we will delete the account.

11. Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be notified in the app.
