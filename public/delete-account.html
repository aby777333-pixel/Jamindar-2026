Delete your Jamin Bazaar account

App: Jamin Bazaar (app.jamindar.mobile) · Developer: Jamin Property Developers · Last updated 5 August 2026

You can ask us to delete your account and the data attached to it at any time, and you do not need the app installed to do it.

How to request deletion

Send us a request from the mobile number or email address on the account, so we can be sure it is you:

Email info@jaminproperties.com with the subject Delete my Jamin Bazaar account

Phone or WhatsApp +91 93848 18895

Please include the mobile number you sign in with, and your Member ID if you have it to hand (you will find it on the Account screen, in the form JA000000).

We will confirm the request, verify that it comes from the account holder, and complete the deletion within 30 days. You will get a confirmation once it is done.

What gets deleted

Deleting the account permanently removes:

  • Your sign-in and profile — mobile number, name, email, photograph, city and language
  • Your KYC submission, including the PAN, Aadhaar, bank and nominee details and every uploaded document
  • Your saved projects, preferences, enquiries, site-visit bookings and in-app messages
  • Your community posts and comments
  • Your referral records and your promoter profile, digital card and earning tree position
  • Your voice conversations with the Jamindar assistant

What we may keep, and why

A small amount of information survives deletion because the law requires it or because it is no longer yours alone:

WhatWhyHow long
Completed sale and commission recordsTax, accounting and audit obligationsAs required by Indian law, typically 8 years
Anonymised usage countsCannot be linked back to you once your account is goneIndefinitely
Messages you sent to another memberThey are part of that person's conversation tooShown without your profile attached

If you only want to remove some things

You do not have to delete the whole account to clean up:

  • Profile photograph — Account → Edit profile → Remove. The stored file is deleted, not merely hidden.
  • A community post — open it and delete it.
  • Saved projects — tap the heart again on anything in your wishlist.
  • Correct your details — Account → Edit profile.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you are a verified partner, deleting the account also gives up your Promoter ID, your referral code and your position in the earning tree. Commission already earned and approved will still be paid to you.
