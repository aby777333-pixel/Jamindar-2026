Delete your account

App: Jamin Bazaar (app.jamindar.mobile) · Jamin Properties I PVT LTD · Updated 16 August 2026

You can delete your Jamin Bazaar account and the personal data attached to it at any time. There are two ways to do it — one inside the app, one here, without installing anything.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Jamin Bazaar and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account (below Sign out).
  4. Read what is deleted and what is kept, type DELETE to confirm, and confirm when prompted. Your account and data are removed immediately, and you are signed out.

Request deletion without the app

Email info@jaminbazaar.in from the email address on your account, with the subject line Delete my Jamin Bazaar account, and include the registered mobile number. We verify that the request is genuinely yours, then delete the account within 7 working days and confirm by email. There is no charge.

If you have a booking in progress, tell us in the same email whether you want the booking cancelled. Deleting your account does not by itself cancel a booking or trigger a refund — see the Booking & refund policy.

What is deleted

  • Your login credentials and all active sessions.
  • Your name, mobile number, email address and postal address.
  • All KYC documents you uploaded — identity and address proofs — permanently removed from storage.
  • Payment-proof images and receipts you uploaded, where they are not attached to a completed transaction.
  • Site-visit bookings, saved plots, enquiries and in-app messages.
  • Device tokens, so you stop receiving notifications.

What is retained, and for how long

A small amount of information must be kept even after deletion, because Indian law requires it for property and financial transactions. It is kept in restricted storage, is not used to contact you, and is deleted when the period ends.

RetainedWhyPeriod
Booking, payment and invoice records for a confirmed booking or executed sale agreement Tax, accounting, registration and RERA record-keeping obligations 8 years from the end of the relevant financial year
Identity records where required to evidence a completed property transaction Statutory transaction records Same as above
Records subject to an ongoing dispute, investigation or court order Legal necessity Until the matter concludes
Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics that cannot identify you Service improvement Indefinite

After deletion

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You can register again later with the same mobile number, but it will be a new account with no previous history, and KYC will need to be completed again.
